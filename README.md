# Patrick Schnurbusch

**Data Analyst | Data Science | Automation | GIS | Time-Series Analysis**

I am a data analyst based in Ontario, Canada, with a background in political science and a strong focus on data-driven problem solving. My work combines analytics, automation, machine learning, and geospatial methods to build practical tools, extract insights, and improve decision-making.

I am especially interested in applied data science, business analytics, forecasting, geographic information systems, and automation workflows.
